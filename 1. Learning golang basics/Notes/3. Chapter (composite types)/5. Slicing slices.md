A slice expression creates a slice from a slice
- its written using brackets and consists of starting offset and ending offset with (:) as separation

When you take a slice from a slice, you are not making a copy of the data.
- so if you change something from outer slice, it also affects the inner slices.
- so don't append to slices that are slices of slices. it fucks up memory

To avoid complicated slice situations, you should either never use append with a sub‐
slice or make sure that append doesn’t cause an overwrite by using a full slice expres‐
sion.
